How MedPulse AI Works

Three straightforward steps to bridge the distance between symptom and safe care.

1

1. Describe Symptoms

Speak into the microphone or type how you feel in Tamil, Hindi, or English. Optional questions ask for age group and duration.

2

2. Review Urgency Guidance

Instantly receive preliminary triage category (Emergency Red, Urgent Amber, or Routine Green) with clear, transparent clinical reasoning.

3

3. Find Appropriate Care

Locate the nearest government health center or hospital equipped for your care level, complete with phone numbers, bed capacity, and bus routes.

Bridging Healthcare Disparities in Rural Communities

In rural districts, lack of medical triage often leads to two fatal extremes: critical emergencies delayed until it's too late, or overburdened tertiary hospitals crowded with mild, routine conditions. MedPulse AI acts as a digital first-responder companion for patients and ASHA community health workers.
