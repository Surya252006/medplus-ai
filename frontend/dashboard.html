Privacy & Health Data Safeguards

MedPulse AI is designed with strict healthcare data privacy principles. We do not require accounts or personal phone numbers to use the triage service. Symptom history is only saved locally when you explicitly select the "Save to History" checkbox. You can permanently erase individual records or purge your entire assessment history at any time.
